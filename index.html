How do I place an order?

Message us on WhatsApp at +234 906 807 2310. Tell us the errand, your location and when you need it done, and we'll come back with a quote.

Do you deliver outside Lagos?

We're based in Alaba, Lagos. Message us with your location and we'll tell you straight away whether we can take the errand on.

What do your services cost?

It depends on the errand, the distance and the items involved. We confirm a quote with you on WhatsApp first — nothing starts until you approve it.

It depends on the errand and where it is. Send your request and we'll tell you the earliest we can get it done — we won't promise what we can't deliver.

Describe it in your own words — a photo of the item, the receipt or the prescription is usually enough for us to understand. If it is not something we can do, we will say so.

How do I pay?
